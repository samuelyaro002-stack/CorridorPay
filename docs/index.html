CorridorPay

An agent that helps Web3 teams pay community moderators and ambassadors in Africa in a local stablecoin. USD₮ goes in, converts to cNGN on Textile FX, and lands in the contributor's wallet. Every step is onchain and tagged.

How a payout works

  1. QuoteAsk Textile FX for a firm USD₮ to cNGN quote, signed by the agent wallet. No account or API key.
  2. SwapApprove and swap on Celo. The swap pays the agent wallet itself.
  3. Pay outThe agent reads the exact cNGN the swap delivered from its logs and sends it to the contributor.

Safety checks: payouts are capped, the recipient can't be the agent wallet, a quote that costs more than approved is refused, and a dry run is the default.
