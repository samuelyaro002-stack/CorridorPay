What to know

  
    
    
  • Non-custodial: your wallet signs everything. CorridorPay never holds your funds or keys.
    • 
    
  • It needs USD₮ and a little CELO for network fees on Celo mainnet. Real money moves, so check the recipient address.
    • 
    
  • The swap runs on Textile FX (fee about 2 bps). Every transaction carries the tag celo_046f6be4dc48.
    • 
    
  • Works in a browser with a wallet (MetaMask, Rabby) or inside a wallet app's built-in browser. If your browser blocks Textile directly, price requests go through the CorridorPay desk, which only forwards them.
    • 
    
  • Beta, built for the Celo Agents Open for Business hackathon. Not audited.
